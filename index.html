What is Resonance?

Resonance is a sound-based wellness and mindfulness application built around the Solfeggio frequency tradition, guided routines, and reflective insight tools.

It combines audio sessions, journaling, habit-building structure, and interpretive content such as lunar timing, zodiac views, and Benner-cycle context. These features are intended for personal reflection and general wellbeing rather than medical or financial decision-making.

The app includes a personal 21-day routine builder, instant boosts, a lunar calendar, zodiac-themed views, and tools to track your journey over time.

The "Why" — Science & Reasoning
📅
Why 21 Days?
The neuroscience of habit formation
⌄
The 21-day structure is a product-design choice: long enough to repeat a routine and short enough to review without claiming that a habit or brain change is complete.
21
Days in this guided reflection sequence; not a scientific threshold for habit formation
66
A commonly cited average from one habit study, with substantial person-to-person variation
The plan is structured in three simple product phases for variety and review:

Week 1 — Observe: Notice the routine, your comfort, and any reflection prompts that feel useful.

Week 2 — Build: Repeat the routine and pair listening blocks with practical reflection prompts for confidence and communication.

Week 3 — Review: Decide which practical behaviors, if any, are worth continuing. No automatic state change is promised.
🎵
Why These Specific Frequencies?
Tradition, preference, and evidence boundaries
⌄
The frequency labels in Resonance come from modern spiritual and sound-wellness traditions. They are included as cultural context and listening preferences, not established medical categories.

The app maps tones to reflection themes for navigation and routine design. Those associations are interpretive; they do not establish chakra, emotional, planetary, or biological effects.

Evidence boundary: Small exploratory studies do not establish that a 528 Hz tone repairs DNA, treats a condition, or reliably changes hormones. Resonance presents it only as a listening preference and reflection cue.

432 Hz is offered as an alternative tuning preference. Resonance does not claim that it matches Earth resonances or synchronizes with the brain.
🌅
Why Morning & Night Sessions?
Consistent cues without neurological claims
⌄
Morning and night sessions are offered because those times can be convenient routine cues, not because the app can program the brain.

Morning: Use a short listening block to review priorities or write one practical intention for the day.

Night (before sleep): Low-volume listening can be used as a consistent wind-down cue. The app does not claim access to the subconscious, improved sleep, or physical repair.
2×
Two optional routine windows; no faster result or brainwave optimization is claimed
🎯
Why Set an Intention Before Each Session?
A simple planning and attention cue
⌄
Setting an intention can turn a vague goal into a clear statement that is easier to review after a session.

Resonance does not claim that entering an intention programs a neurological system, changes reality, or attracts an outcome. The field is simply a journaling and planning prompt.

Pairing an intention with a tone may make the routine easier to remember. Outcomes depend on real actions and circumstances, and no accelerated result is promised.
🎧
Why Headphones for Day, Speaker for Sleep?
Binaural processing & ear safety
⌄
Headphones during day sessions: Separate tones presented to each ear can create a perceived binaural beat at their frequency difference. Resonance does not claim that this produces focus, entrainment, or a stronger neurological response. Use a comfortable volume and stop if listening feels unpleasant.

Speaker during rest: If you choose background audio, a low-volume speaker avoids wearing earbuds for a long period. The app does not claim that the body becomes more receptive during sleep or that sound improves sleep.
Low
Keep volume comfortable. This app does not provide a universal safe percentage because output varies by device and headphones.
How to Use Resonance
1
Start on Today. Check your moon phase and pick how you feel. The app suggests frequencies based on your current state.
2
Go to Journey → My Plan. Follow your morning session first thing when you wake up, before checking your phone.
3
Set an intention before each session starts. One sentence. What do you want to feel or achieve?
4
Put headphones on, lie or sit comfortably, close your eyes. The session plays and stops automatically.
5
Rate your session after it completes. Over time your journal shows patterns — which frequencies move you most.
6
Use Quick Boosts in Journey → Boosts for targeted needs throughout the day — stress, focus, energy, confidence.
7
Start 432 Hz overnight in My Space. Speaker on your nightstand, volume barely audible. Let it run all night.
8
Check Cosmos and select a zodiac sign for optional tradition-based listening and lunar reflection prompts.
